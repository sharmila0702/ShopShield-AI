# 🛡️ ShopShield AI

### Your Agentic Shopping Guardian

ShopShield AI is an AI-powered shopping decision assistant designed to help users make smarter purchasing decisions.

Instead of simply recommending products, ShopShield AI evaluates a user's requirements, budget, product ratings, performance, and potential purchase risk before giving a final recommendation.

## 🚀 Features

- 🧠 Requirement Analysis
- 🛍️ Product Evaluation
- ⚖️ Product Comparison
- 💰 Budget Analysis
- ⚠️ Purchase Risk Analysis
- 😟 Buyer Regret Score
- 🎯 Explainable Purchase Decision
- ✅ BUY / CONSIDER / AVOID recommendation

## 🤖 Agent Workflow

## 😟 Buyer Regret Score

ShopShield AI provides a prototype **Buyer Regret Score** from 0–100.

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| 0–30 | 🟢 Low Regret |
| 31–60 | 🟡 Medium Regret |
| 61–100 | 🔴 High Regret |

The score is a prototype estimate based on factors such as budget usage and product rating. It is not a scientifically validated prediction.

## 🎯 Objective

To develop an AI-powered agentic commerce assistant that understands user requirements, evaluates products based on budget and preferences, compares alternatives, identifies potential purchase risks, and provides an explainable BUY, CONSIDER, or AVOID decision.
